The solution

One link. One click. A record that holds.

Signoff turns "looks good I guess" into a formal approval you can point to when the next request comes in.

Staging URLs as deliverables

Attach staging links, screenshots and notes to each milestone. Built for how developers actually ship.

No client login

Your client gets a unique link. No account, no password, no app to download. They just review and decide.

Timestamped sign-off

Every approval records the client's name, date and time. No more "I never agreed to that."

All projects, one dashboard

See every client project and the status of each milestone at a glance: waiting, in feedback, approved.

How it works

From staging link to signed off in four steps

  1. 01

    Define your milestones

    Create a project and split it into milestones, each with a title, a description and an amount.

  2. 02

    Attach the deliverables

    Add the staging URL, screenshots and notes that show the milestone is done.

  3. 03

    Send one link

    Your client opens a clean review page and clicks Approve or Leave feedback.

  4. 04

    Get it on record

    The approval is timestamped and stored. Anything new after that is a separate request, so you can bill it.
